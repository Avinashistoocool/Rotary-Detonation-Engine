What combines into credible evidence

Synchronized, high-bandwidth circumferential pressure measurements support propagation direction, wave count and speed.

Time-resolved optical diagnostics, where feasible, can help distinguish the shock and reaction structure. Combine these with mixture-specific detonation references, uncertainty and repeatability.

Supply/flow records, thermal measurements and as-built configuration establish context; calibrated thrust measurements support performance claims, not detonation identification by themselves.

What does not establish it

Glow, noise, a single oscillating pressure signal or a pleasing animation are not sufficient proof of rotating detonation.

Speed near an ideal reference is supporting evidence, not a stand-alone certificate. CFD agreement alone cannot establish an experimental wave mode.

No data exists in this illustration. Actual evidence and acceptance must be reviewed; a negative demonstration result remains valid.
